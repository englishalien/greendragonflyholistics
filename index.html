What GDH therapies

Therapies

Find your way to calm.
Relaxation looks different for everyone. Some people need to quieten a busy mind, others need to feel held and restored, and some simply need their body to melt. Each of our services offers its own route to rest, whether through sound, stillness or touch. Choose the one that speaks to you, or explore them together for a deeper, more rounded reset.

Sound Bath

Let the sound do the work.
Lie back and be enveloped by the deep, resonant tones of singing bowls, gongs and other therapeutic instruments. You don’t need to focus, meditate or “get it right”. The vibrations move through the room and through you, gently pulling a busy mind out of its loops and into the present.

Best for: anyone who finds it hard to switch off, or who’s tried meditation and struggled to quieten their thoughts. The sound gives your mind something soothing to follow, so stillness comes more easily.
You’ll leave feeling: mentally clear, lighter and refreshed, as if you’ve had a deep reset.

"Pause, breathe, and let the sounds carry you into stillness."

Reiki

Quiet, restorative stillness.
A calm, hands-on-or-just-above-the-body practice rooted in Japanese tradition, Reiki is about being held in stillness. There’s no music to follow and nothing to process, just a peaceful space where you can rest and receive. Many people find sessions especially grounding when life feels emotionally heavy or you simply need to feel looked after.

Best for: moments when you’re feeling overwhelmed, depleted or emotionally stretched, and need a gentle, nurturing space to settle and rebalance.
You’ll leave feeling: calm, grounded and cared for, with a softer, more settled emotional state.

"A moment of calm, a space to reconnect, and time to nurture yourself from within."

ASMR Massage

Relaxation you can feel on your skin.
This is the most sensory of the three. Feather-light strokes, delicate brushing and soft, rhythmic movements trigger that slow, tingling, melt-into-the-bed feeling many people recognise from ASMR. It’s a different route to relaxation: through touch, rather than sound or stillness.

Best for: anyone carrying physical tension or who struggles to relax until they’re really feeling something soothing. It’s also ideal if you’re curious about ASMR and want to experience it in person.
You’ll leave feeling: deeply relaxed in your body, with tension eased and a heavy, blissful calm.

"Switch off from the outside world, settle into the moment, and let yourself drift into deep relaxation."

Why not combine therapies?

Each service relaxes you in a different way: sound for the mind, Reiki for the emotions, ASMR touch for the body. Many clients enjoy alternating between them, or pairing them for a full-spectrum reset. Ask about combining services to find the perfect blend for you.

Who's behind Green Dragonfly Holistics?

Hello, I’m Linda.

I spent years as a project analyst, a world of deadlines, data and a brain that never quite switched off. Then, completely by accident, I ended up at a gong bath.

It was part of a yoga retreat day, and I hadn’t even spotted it on the schedule when I booked. Twenty minutes in, my muscles had melted, my stubborn hips had finally let go, and my mind had drifted somewhere I never knew it could go. I walked out blown away, and a little bit changed.

Why I trained

After that first gong, I went looking for more. Every sound bath left me feeling softer, calmer and more at ease than I’d felt in years.

Then I was offered redundancy. Instead of panicking, I took it as the universe nudging me towards something more meaningful: helping other people feel the way I’d felt that day.

A one-day sound healing course left me with more questions than answers, so I went in search of a proper teacher. I trained with SoundSphere Scotland on the six-month Alembic Sound diploma, a mix of hands-on case studies, the science behind sound, and plenty of time playing the instruments. I finished with a distinction, and I’ve never looked back. Reiki came next, with Levels 1 and 2 over eight months with Reiki Evolution, followed by ASMR massage training with a hands-on practical assessment.

Three treatments, one idea

I chose sound, Reiki and ASMR touch because they work beautifully together. Sound quietens the mind, Reiki settles the emotions and ASMR melts the body, so between them there’s a route to relaxation for everyone. You can book them separately, or I’ll happily blend them into a session made just for you.

"Relaxation is a process, not a one-time fix. But even a single session can ripple through the rest of your day, your week and beyond."

There’s no wrong way to relax

If I could change one thing, it would be the idea that you have to be “good” at resting. Your brain might race. Your body might cling to tension. That doesn’t mean you’re doing it wrong, and it doesn’t mean the treatment isn’t working.

What to expect

My space is warm, calm and welcoming, and you’ll be in good hands from the moment you arrive. First-timers often walk in nervous, and that’s completely normal. Most people find it melts away within minutes.

Clients have described my energy as gentle and said I’m good at putting them at ease. They leave deeply relaxed, and several have told me they sleep brilliantly afterwards.

Practising what I preach (when I can)

I’ll be honest: even with a business built around rest, I’m not always brilliant at making time for it myself. But I try to start every morning with ten minutes of meditation or gentle movement, even on the busiest days. I’ve been strength training for years (and keep regular sessions with my PT, because without them I probably wouldn’t go!). Yoga is my real saviour. I look forward to my weekly class with a small, friendly group of women, and it’s where I switch off best.

It keeps me grounded, and it reminds me that looking after yourself is something we all have to keep choosing.

Ready when you are

Whether you need quiet, comfort or a full-body melt, I’d love to welcome you in. Book a session and find the kind of calm that works for you.
